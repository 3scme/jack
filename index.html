14 JUNE WED.

Broke camp at 0640. Low cloud along the eastern ridge, clearing before nine. Followed the creek north to the shale exposure marked yesterday.

Bed dips roughly 18° west. Dark stone beneath the shale is harder than expected. Sample 14-C taken approximately thirty yards east of the creek.
